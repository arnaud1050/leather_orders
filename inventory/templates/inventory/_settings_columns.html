Inventory list columns

Reorder and hide/show the columns shown on the Inventory list. Drag a row by its handle to reorder — it saves as you drop it. Hidden columns keep their data; they just stop rendering that column, so hiding Notes or Ref is a way to keep the table narrow without losing anything. Name is always shown — it's what opens an item's edit form.
