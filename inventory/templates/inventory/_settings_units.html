Units

What quantities are measured in — shown as a dropdown when adding or editing an inventory item. Picked from a fixed list rather than typed freely, so the Materials tab always knows whether a quantity is whole (like Each or Skein) or divisible (like Sqft or Yard). Each is always available and can't be hidden or removed. Drag a row by its handle to reorder — it saves as you drop it. A unit can't be deleted once an item is measured in it — hide it instead to remove it from new selections while keeping that item labelled correctly.
