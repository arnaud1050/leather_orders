Document types

Optional categories for a document (e.g. Mockups, Renderings) — once at least one exists, an order's Documents area splits into a section per type, in the order set here, each with its own upload button. With none defined, documents stay in one flat list, as they do today. Drag a row by its handle to reorder. A type can't be deleted once a document has been filed under it — hide it instead to remove it from new uploads while keeping that document's section.
